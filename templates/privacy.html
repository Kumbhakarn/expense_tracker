Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic usage data — information such as login times, used to keep your account secure.

We do not connect to your bank accounts, and we do not collect payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve Spendly's features.
  • Show you summaries and breakdowns of your spending.
  • Secure your account and prevent misuse of the service.
  • Contact you about important changes to the service or these policies.

We never sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers, and we take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Passwords are stored only in hashed form.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal and expense data will be permanently removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers, such as hosting services, to operate the app. These providers process data only on our behalf and are required to keep it secure and confidential. We do not share your data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to access, correct, or delete your data, please contact us at privacy@spendly.app.
